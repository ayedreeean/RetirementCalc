Age 45

How much do I need to retire at 45?

For $70,000 a year, 25 times spending is $1,750,000. A retirement from 45 to 90 is 45 years. This table has 6 complete windows of that length. 6 of 6 complete windows kept a balance above zero at the end of every year. Shuffled years on the same inputs: 957 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon.

Methodology

Forty-five is a common early-retirement target because it is far enough from a traditional career to matter, and the saving years from a mid-30s start are still a decade rather than two. The dollars are still spending times a multiple, and the multiple is only as good as the horizon you test.

On seed 246813, the median path takes 11 years, age 45. The faster 10% are there by 9 years, age 43. The slower 10% take at least 14 years, age 48. 1,000 of 1,000 shuffled paths reached the goal inside 50 years. The median lands on age 45. The slower 10% land at age 48 or later, which is not 45. 40 of 50 start years reach the goal before the sample ends. The median of all 50 start years is 11 years. The faster 10% are there by 9 years. Starts near 2024 have only a few years of data, so the slower tail includes windows that run out of history. Plan closer to the slower figure if missing the birthday would break the plan.

If you want the same spending with a lower rate, divide $70,000 by 0.035. That portfolio is $2,000,000. This page does not run that larger balance. The withdrawal-rate guide compares the rates on $1 million so the only change is the spending.

Questions people ask next

How many historical windows is 45 years?

6 complete windows, 1975–2019 through 1980–2024. That is the whole sample. It is not a full-history study of every US retirement.

Does the example savings rate hit 45?

On seed 246813, the median path takes 11 years, age 45. The faster 10% are there by 9 years. The slower 10% take at least 14 years.

Is $1.75 million enough if I spend more than $70,000?

No. The portfolio is 25 times $70,000. Higher spending is a higher withdrawal rate, or a higher goal. Change the spending field and read the new count.

What should I open first, savings or retirement?

Savings, if you do not have the portfolio yet. Retirement, if you do. A balance that reaches the goal can still run out later. Both links are on this page.
