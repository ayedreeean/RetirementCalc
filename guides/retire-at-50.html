How much do I need to retire at 50?

For $80,000 a year, a 4% sketch is $2,000,000. From 50 to 90 is 40 years, and this table has 11 complete 40-year windows. 11 of 11 complete windows kept a balance above zero at the end of every year. 963 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon. Spending $70,000 instead, a 3.5% draw on the same portfolio, kept a balance on 981 of 1,000 shuffled paths.

Methodology

Age 50 is often the first birthday where a household both has a large portfolio and still faces a retirement longer than the 30-year studies. The calculator can hold that horizon. It cannot invent earnings history, a pension, or a health premium. Those have to be inside the numbers you type, or left off on purpose.

From 40 to the $2 million goal

Age 40, $520,000 invested, $150,000 of income, $80,000 of spending. The gap is $70,000 a year. Real raises are 1%. Stocks are 70%. The goal is $2,000,000 in today’s dollars, 25× spending.

On seed 246813, the median path takes 10 years, age 50. The faster 10% are there by 7 years, age 47. The slower 10% take at least 13 years, age 53. 1,000 of 1,000 shuffled paths reached the goal inside 50 years. The median age is 50. The slower 10% are 53 or older. 41 of 50 start years reach the goal before the sample ends. The median of all 50 start years is 10 years. The faster 10% are there by 8 years. Starts near 2024 have only a few years of data, so the slower tail includes windows that run out of history.

A 40-year retirement at 4%, and at a lower spend

The withdrawal example is age 50, $2,000,000, $80,000, 60% stocks, 0% tax, inflation on, Social Security off. 11 of 11 complete windows kept a balance above zero at the end of every year. 963 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon. The earliest shuffled failure lasted 19 years.

The third button keeps $2,000,000 and spends $70,000. That is 3.5%. 981 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon. Same history, same mix, lower bills. If your spending cannot fall, the matching move is a larger portfolio, which is the savings question again.

Questions people ask next

How is retiring at 50 different from retiring at 65 in this tool?

The horizon. A 30-year retirement has 21 complete windows here. A 40-year retirement has 11. The extra decade is the part a standard 4% write-up does not cover.

Does this savings example make it by 50?

On seed 246813, the median path takes 10 years, age 50. The faster 10% are there by 7 years. The slower 10% take at least 13 years.

What does spending $70,000 change?

It is 3.5% of $2 million instead of 4%. On the shuffled paths, the count moves from 963 of 1,000 to 981 of 1,000.

Should I turn Social Security on?

If you want the success count to include a benefit, yes, and then say so when you quote the result. This page leaves it off. Someone retiring at 50 still has a long gap before a claim at 67.
