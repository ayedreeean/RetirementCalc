Questions people ask next

Do these pages calculate on a server?

No. A guide is static HTML. The button is a share link. The browser runs the plan.

Which page is the 4% rule?

The 4% rule guide tests a textbook 30-year case and the six stress packs. The safe withdrawal rate guide compares 4%, 3.5%, 3.25%, and 3% on one portfolio.

Where is Coast FIRE?

The Coast FIRE guide explains the zero-contribution check. The Coast FIRE calculator opens that example directly.

Is this financial advice?

No. FIREcalc is a planning sketch. The limits of the data set are on every page and in the methodology note.
