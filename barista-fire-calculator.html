Barista FIRE calculator

Enter the full budget as spending and the job as other income. On this example the job is $1,500 a month for 15 years. Without it, 4 of 6 historical windows survived. With it, 6 of 6 did. Shuffled paths moved from 520 of 1,000 to 745 of 1,000.

The paycheck does not rise with inflation, and it stops after 15 years. Spending does rise with inflation. Change the monthly amount and the duration to match the job you would actually do.

745 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon. That is with the job. The guide explains why two historical windows ran out when the job was removed, and why a nominal paycheck shrinks against an inflation-adjusted budget. Health insurance that depends on the job is not priced here. Add that premium to spending if losing the job would mean paying it yourself.

Questions people ask next

Which fields does the button set?

Retirement age 45, $700,000, $48,000 spending, 60% stocks, 0% tax, 45-year horizon, other income $1,500 a month for 15 years, Social Security off.

What failed without the income?

The 1976 and 1977 windows. 520 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon.

Can the job last the whole retirement?

Set the duration to 0. In this tool, 0 means the other income continues for the whole horizon. It still will not rise with inflation.

Is this Coast FIRE?

No. Coast FIRE is zero new savings before you retire. This page is a retirement that still has a paycheck. The coast calculator is a separate page.
