Lean FIRE calculator

Lean FIRE in this tool is a small spending number. The example is $32,000 a year and an $800,000 portfolio, a 4% draw, for 45 years. 6 of 6 complete windows kept a balance above zero at the end of every year. 957 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon.

Six historical windows are a thin sample for a 45-year retirement. The shuffled count is the wider stress, and it is not a historical cohort. The guide also times a savings path from age 33 toward the $800,000 goal. Fat FIRE is the same engine at a high budget, on the fat FIRE page.

Keep the spending number honest. A lean plan that only works if you later spend more is a different plan. This is a sketch, not advice.

Questions people ask next

What is prefilled?

Age 45, $800,000, $32,000 of spending, 70% stocks, 0% tax, 45-year horizon, Social Security off, historical cycles. The second button changes only the spending, to $28,000, and uses shuffled years.

How often did $28,000 survive the shuffle?

983 of 1,000 shuffled paths kept a balance above zero for every year of the horizon. The $32,000 shuffle was 957 of 1,000.

Does a lower budget need a different data set?

No. The same 1975–2024 stock and Treasury table runs every plan. The sample still omits 1966 and 1973–74.

How do I model getting to $800,000?

Use the savings button on the lean FIRE guide. This page is the withdrawal test.
