Coast FIRE calculator

Coast FIRE means the portfolio you already have can reach your number with no new savings. This calculator does that check by setting income equal to spending and real raises to 0%. It does not solve for the balance you would need today. On the example, seed 246813, the median path takes 16 years.

Methodology

Set the goal to the portfolio you want in today’s dollars, often 25 times the spending you expect after you stop working. Set today’s income equal to today’s spending. Set real raises to 0%. The contribution stays at zero because both sides rise only with CPI. The median years are how long this balance takes with market returns alone.

On seed 246813, the median path takes 16 years, age 56. The faster 10% are there by 10 years, age 50. The slower 10% take at least 28 years, age 68. 997 of 1,000 shuffled paths reached the goal inside 50 years. 33 of 50 start years reach the goal before the sample ends. The median of all 50 start years is 20 years. The faster 10% are there by 12 years. Starts near 2024 have only a few years of data, so the slower tail includes windows that run out of history. The slower 10% at age 68 are the figure to respect if you needed the money sooner. The full guide walks the same example and explains why the model will not print a coast number.

When the date arrives, test the withdrawal. Coast FIRE is about getting to the number. It is not the retirement. This is a planning sketch, not advice.

Questions people ask next

Does this solve for my Coast FIRE number?

No. Change “invested today” and run it again until the slower paths arrive on time. The tool times a balance. It does not search for one.

What did the example do?

Age 40, $450,000, income and spending both $85,000, goal $1,500,000, 80% stocks. The median path takes 16 years. The faster 10% are there by 10 years. The slower 10% take at least 28 years. 997 of 1,000 shuffled paths reached the goal.

What about historical cycles?

33 of 50 start years reach the goal before the sample ends. The median of all 50 start years is 20 years.

Is barista FIRE on this page?

No. A coast check has no withdrawals yet. Part-time income during retirement is the barista guide, using other income on the retirement calculator.
