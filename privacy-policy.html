Privacy Policy for FIREcalc

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Welcome to FIREcalc! This Privacy Policy explains how FIREcalc ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with your use of the FIREcalc application (the "App"). Your privacy is important to us.

1. Information We Collect (and What We Don't)

FIREcalc is designed with your privacy in mind.

  • Calculator Input Data: When you use the Savings Simulator or Retirement Simulator, you provide financial information such as your age, current savings, income, expenses, retirement goals, and investment strategies ("Calculator Data"). The simulations run locally in your browser. We do not send Calculator Data to an AI service, and we do not store it on our servers. The app may save your inputs in your browser's local storage so the form can be restored, and a share link puts those inputs in the page URL. A share link can appear in your browser history and in ordinary hosting logs.
  • No Personal Accounts: FIREcalc does not require you to create a user account or provide personal information like your name or email address to use its core calculator functionalities.
  • Usage Data (Standard Web Logs): Like most websites and online services, our hosting provider (e.g., Cloudflare) may collect standard technical information when you access the App online (e.g., via firecalc.ai). This may include IP addresses, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited. This information is typically aggregated and used for statistical purposes, to maintain service security, and to improve the App. This data is subject to the privacy policy of our hosting provider and is not directly linked by us to your specific Calculator Data.
  • Analytics:
    • Google Analytics 4: We use Google Analytics 4 on the public FIREcalc site (https://firecalc.ai) to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics may collect page views, device and browser information, approximate geographic region, and the referring website or URL. Google Analytics uses cookies (or similar technologies) to distinguish browsers and measure visits. We use this information in aggregate to understand traffic and improve the App. We do not send your Calculator Data, name, email, or other personal identifiers through Google Analytics, and we do not link these analytics signals to your Calculator Data. Google's processing of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can also read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
    • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics may collect privacy-friendly usage metrics for the hosted site, such as page views and general traffic patterns. These metrics are not linked by us to your Calculator Data. Cloudflare's handling of this information is governed by Cloudflare's privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide App Functionality: Your Calculator Data is used locally on your device to perform the financial simulations and display the results and charts you request within the App. Inputs saved in local storage stay on that device.
  • To Improve the App: Aggregated usage information from Google Analytics 4, Cloudflare Web Analytics, and standard web logs may be used to understand how people use the App, to monitor performance, and to guide improvements. This usage information is separate from your Calculator Data, which stays on your device.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your Calculator Data.

  • Service Providers: We use third-party service providers for hosting and analytics. Cloudflare hosts the site and may collect standard web logs and privacy-friendly Web Analytics metrics. Google Analytics 4 receives the analytics signals described above (page views, device and browser, approximate region, and referrer). These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not share your Calculator Data with them. If you open a share link, the query string is part of that request.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect and defend our rights or property, prevent fraud, act in urgent circumstances to protect the personal safety of users of the App, or protect against legal liability.

4. Data Security

  • Calculator Data: Simulations run locally. Inputs kept in local storage are only as private as the browser profile on that device. Share links contain the inputs in the URL.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect any information we handle, but no security system is impenetrable.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics 4: The public site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google. It may record page views, device and browser type, approximate region, and referrer. You can review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: The FIREcalc website and PWA are hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare Web Analytics may also collect privacy-friendly usage metrics. You can review Cloudflare's privacy policy on their website.
  • The App may contain links to other websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of such other sites or services.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or on our website. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted.
